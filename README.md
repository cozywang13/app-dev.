## B. Synopsis

*Summary:*
Badland Hunters is a South Korean action movie set in a post-apocalyptic world where a massive earthquake has destroyed Seoul and turned the city into a dangerous wasteland. People struggle to survive because of the lack of food, water, and safety. The story follows Nam-san, a brave hunter who lives in the ruined city and protects the people around him. One day, a teenage girl named Su-na is kidnapped by a group of people working for a dangerous doctor who conducts strange experiments on humans. Nam-san decides to rescue her and faces many powerful enemies along the way. As he fights through dangerous situations, he discovers the doctor's dark plans and risks his life to save Su-na. The movie shows the importance of courage, survival, friendship, and protecting innocent people even in the most difficult situations.

3. *Yang Gi-su* – A mysterious doctor who conducts dangerous human experiments. He wants to find a way to achieve immortality.
4. *Lee Eun-ho* – A military sergeant who helps Nam-san rescue Su-na and fights against the people responsible for the experiments.
